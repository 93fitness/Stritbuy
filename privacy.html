Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

StritBuy (stritbuy.com) helps people in Nigeria find local shops and services near them. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who sees it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who we are

The operator of StritBuy is [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us"). Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider, so we cannot read it.
  • Location: the state, town and area you choose to browse or list in.
  • Your activity: items you save, shops you follow, a log of the shops you contacted through the site, and ratings you give.
  • Seller content: shop name, address, phone or WhatsApp number, opening hours, product names, prices and photos you upload. This is shown publicly.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address and browser type, which our hosting and service providers record to run and protect the service.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and show listings (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To send emails you ask for, such as password resets (contract and your request).
  • To keep the site safe, stop abuse and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To follow the law.
  • Where we ask for it, with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Who can see your data

  • Public: everything a seller publishes in a shop listing, including the shop's phone number.
  • Private: your email, password, saved items, followed shops and contact log are visible only to you and to us as the operator.
  • When you call or WhatsApp a shop, the shop sees your phone number through your phone or WhatsApp, not through us.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not show advertising.

5. Service providers

We use trusted companies to run StritBuy: Supabase (database, login and photo storage), Render (website hosting), Resend (emails) and Spaceship (domain). They process data for us under their own terms. Some of them store data outside Nigeria. Where we transfer data abroad, we do so as the NDPA allows.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within a reasonable time, except where we must keep something to follow the law or deal with abuse. Copies in backups are removed on a normal backup cycle.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, receive a copy of it, and withdraw your consent. To use any right, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections and access rules in our database. No system is completely secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.

9. Children

StritBuy is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage only to keep you signed in and make the site work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for big changes, tell you on the site or by email.
